## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined Mediterranean sanctuary, blending the artisanal intimacy of specialty coffee culture in Benicarló with elevated editorial hospitality. The aesthetic balances deep, tactile espresso tones with radiant, creamy parchment paper textures. 

### Design Principles & Visual Mood
- **Warm Editorial Sophistication:** Evoking high-end print menus, botanical engravings, and architectural arches rather than generic cafe interfaces.
- **Jeweled Contrasts:** Anchored by velvety espresso darks, warmed by artisanal creams, and accented by sapphire brilliance borrowed from the logo's central gem.
- **Organic Geometry:** Subtle arched frames, circular seals, delicate hairlines, and tactile blind-deboss elements that mirror the physical brand stamp.

## Colors

The palette embraces a dramatic dual-rhythm: sections alternate between dark roasted espresso canvases and illuminated cream surfaces.

- **Primary (`#1E1713` - Espresso Roast):** Serves as the primary canvas background in dark mode, deep text on light containers, and structural anchors.
- **Secondary (`#F3EBDD` - Warm Cream Parchment):** Used for elevated cards, inverted editorial editorial banners, button fills on dark backgrounds, and warm typographic body copy on espresso layers.
- **Tertiary (`#2F3E9E` - Sapphire Gem):** A jewel-like focal point reserved for primary actions, active navigational states, signature badges, and premium indicators.
- **Neutral (`#7C8B6F` - Botanical Sage):** Evokes coffee plant foliage and coastal flora; applied to delicate borders, secondary badges, ingredient tags, and organic accents.

### Surface Alternation & Texture Rules
When alternating between espresso (`#1E1713`) and warm cream (`#F3EBDD`) sections, borders use matching low-opacity tints (`rgba(243, 235, 221, 0.12)` on dark, and `rgba(30, 23, 19, 0.08)` on light). Subdued botanical watermarks or debossed concentric rings should accompany section breaks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates classic editorial grace with modern utility:

- **Playfair Display:** Chosen for editorial authority and crisp, high-contrast serif strokes that reflect traditional menu craftsmanship. It powers grand display titles and italicized narrative subtitles.
- **EB Garamond:** Warm, intellectual, and literary; renders body copy, culinary descriptions, and sensory origin stories with natural bookish flow.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Crisp, geometric, and clean; applied to micro-copy, nutritional specs, pricing, and interactive controls with wide tracking for legibility.
- **Editorial Accents:** Emphasize quotes, welcome notes ('Te esperamos'), and seasonal recommendations using italicized serif variants framed by delicate hairline rules.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column editorial grid system on desktop, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

Generous whitespace and deliberate margins establish an unhurried, luxury cafe atmosphere:
- Desktop sections leverage wide vertical breathing spaces (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to 5rem) to evoke open book spreads.
- Arched cards and circular vignettes respect offset layouts, allowing featured blends or seasonal bakery items to overlap section boundaries gracefully.
- Fluid column constraints max out at 1280px to preserve comfortable reading lengths for narrative menu copy.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy steers away from generic dropshadows, favoring tactile relief, paper debossing, and ambient glowing contours:

- **Debossed Base Surfaces:** Input fields, card insets, and list containers employ subtle interior bevels and faint interior shadows (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` on dark, `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 23, 19, 0.06)` on light) mimicking stamped cardstock.
- **Sapphire Ambient Glow:** Interactive highlights and hero focal badges project a restrained, tinted luminescence: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(47, 62, 158, 0.28)`.
- **Refined Ghost Hairlines:** Elevation boundaries are delineated by 1px rules tinted with botanical sage (`rgba(124, 139, 111, 0.25)`) or muted gold-cream tones, grounding each element without heavy dimensional projection.

## Shapes

The shape system merges architectural curves with functional softly-rounded interfaces:

- **Architectural Arches:** Hero image frames, featured product highlights, and category banners employ continuous top-arch geometry (`border-radius: 999px 999px 1rem 1rem`).
- **Circular Emblems:** Badges, avatar rings, and seal stamps use full circular geometry (`rounded-full`), directly echoing the round debossed brand emblem.
- **Component Geometry:** Interactive cards, inputs, and dialogs adhere to Level 2 (8px base radius, scaling to 16px on cards), balancing modern usability with timeless craftsmanship.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Sapphire CTA):** Fill of `#2F3E9E`, text in `#F3EBDD`, uppercase `label-lg` typography with tracking. Delicate subtle border `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`. Radius 0.5rem. Hover state elevates slightly with a soft sapphire aura.
- **Secondary Action (Cream Pill):** Fill of `#F3EBDD`, text in `#1E1713`. Subdued and tactile.
- **Tertiary (Ghost Hairline):** Transparent background with 1px border in `rgba(124, 139, 111, 0.4)` and cream text, transitioning to solid sage on hover.

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Arched Feature Card:** Arched top border with an embossed perimeter hairline. Displays single-origin coffee provenance, price in clean sans-serif, and title in Playfair Display.
- **Tasting Menu Row:** Border-bottom hairline dividing menu items; name on the left, dotted leader line or whitespace gap, and price pinned right with sapphire accent tag.

### Chips & Tags
- **Origin & Dietary Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags with 1px border in sage (`#7C8B6F`), soft translucent background (`rgba(124, 139, 111, 0.12)`), text set in `label-md` uppercase.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- **Artisanal Field:** Inset espresso-toned background (`#17110E`), 1px outline in muted cream/sage (`rgba(243, 235, 221, 0.16)`). Focus triggers a crisp `#2F3E9E` border glow with label transitioning into delicate Playfair italic floating text.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular radio rings with a sapphire center pip mimicking the brand's gemstone. Checkboxes feature botanical sage check marks on dark inset backgrounds.
